\section{Known limit on the headline}

The independent ground-truth oracle covered \textbf{22 of the 45} adjudicated
cases, all on the Lagrangian pathway. The other 23 --- every Hamiltonian case
(14), every constrained case (8), and one Lagrangian case where it did not
apply --- rest on energy, constraint, and structural checks alone.

\vspace{0.3em}
State the result \textbf{by pathway}, not pooled. Extending the oracle to the
Hamiltonian pathway is the first thing to do \emph{after} the study closes.
